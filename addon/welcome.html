Welcome to YT Music Cookie

Three steps and your YouTube Music login goes to Music Assistant in one tap.

1 · Allow private windows

Checking…

On desktop: open the Add-ons page (Ctrl+Shift+A), choose YT Music Cookie, and set Run in Private Windows to Allow.

On Android: open Firefox's menu > Add-ons, tap YT Music Cookie, and turn on Run in private browsing.

2 · Connect Music Assistant

Find your server, then sign in through Home Assistant or a Music Assistant account.

3 · Send your login

  1. Go to music.youtube.com and sign in. A normal window is fine.
  2. Open any playlist in your library.
  3. Press the add-on's button, then Send to Music Assistant.
  4. On a computer, turn on Automatic updates in the settings and Music Assistant's copy stays fresh by itself.
